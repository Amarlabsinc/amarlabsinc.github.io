Privacy

This page covers amarlabs.net and our mail. Each app has its own policy, because each app is different; they are linked at the bottom.

Effective 20 September 2026

This website

amarlabs.net is a set of static files. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and carries no advertising or tracking scripts of any kind. There is nothing here to log you in to and nothing here that follows you afterwards.

It makes no request to any third party while you read it. The typeface is served from this domain rather than from a font service, and the images are files on this site, so loading a page tells nobody but our host that you were here.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the requests your browser makes — the address you asked for, your IP address, your browser's user-agent string and the time — and keeps them briefly in its own server logs for security and abuse handling. We cannot see those logs, we do not collect them, and we never combine them with anything else.

Writing to us

When you email info@amarlabs.net, we receive what you send: your address, your message, and anything you attach. We use it to answer you and to fix whatever you have told us about.

The mailbox is hosted by Apple as part of iCloud Mail, so your message sits on Apple's mail servers the way any email sits on the receiving mail provider's servers. We keep support correspondence while it is useful — to recognise a repeat problem, or to follow up on a bug you reported — and delete it when it is not.

We never add you to a mailing list from a support email, never sell or share your address, and never use your message to train an AI model.

Our apps

An app can do things a website cannot, so each one states its own case in full: what it keeps on your device, what it sends anywhere, and who else is involved.

  • Hukam privacy policy

Common to all of them: no advertising, no advertising trackers, no profiling, nothing about you sold or shared for marketing, and nothing you do used to train any AI model.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy of it, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to info@amarlabs.net and we will answer.

In practice the answer is usually short, because there is usually nothing: the apps work without an account, and this website collects nothing at all.
