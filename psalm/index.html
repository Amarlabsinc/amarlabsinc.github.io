Psalm

The Bible on your Home Screen and Lock Screen: a verse for every day, the day's readings for Catholic and Orthodox readers, and the whole Bible to read.

Support

Every word of Scripture in Psalm is free, and always will be.

Questions, problems or requests: email info@amarlabs.net and you will hear back from a person, usually within a couple of days.

If something is broken, tell us the app's version, your iPhone model and your iOS version, and what you did before it went wrong. A screenshot is worth all three.

Where the words come from

  • Scripture is the World English Bible, British Edition, which is in the public domain.
  • Orthodox readings follow the calendar computed by orthocal (MIT licence).
  • Catholic readings follow the US lectionary as gathered by catholic-readings-api (MIT licence). Psalm shows the references and reads them from the World English Bible, so verse numbers can differ from a missalette.
  • The order in which famous verses come first uses cross-references from OpenBible.info (CC BY).
