Stepnaut Privacy Policy

Stepnaut turns the steps you walk into a journey across the solar system. It was built to know as little about you as possible, and your health data never leaves your iPhone. This policy explains what the app does, and mostly what it doesn't do, with your information.

Effective 24 November 2026.

The short version

  • Your steps and walking distance from Apple Health stay on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and never used for advertising.
  • No ads, no advertising trackers, no profiling. Nothing about you is ever sold or shared for marketing.
  • There is no account to create, and Stepnaut has no servers of its own.
  • One third party helps us count subscriptions. It is named below, with exactly what it receives, and it never learns your name, your email address or anything from Apple Health.
  • We never use anything you do in Stepnaut to train any AI model.

Apple Health

With your permission, Stepnaut reads two things from Apple Health: your step count and your walking and running distance. It uses them only to fly your ship, keep your streak, and fill your calendar, stats, widgets and Lock Screen countdown.

Stepnaut never writes anything to Apple Health. What it reads is used on your iPhone and is never sent to Amar Labs, to RevenueCat or to anyone else, never used for advertising, and never sold. You can change or remove Stepnaut's access at any time in the Health app or in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.

What stays on your device

Your answers on the welcome screens (why you walk, your daily goal), the names you give your captain and your robot, your ships, souvenirs, settings and reminder times are kept on your iPhone and are never sent to us.

Subscriptions

Stepnaut Premium is billed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details, and whether you have Premium is decided on your device from Apple's own record of what you bought, not by any server of ours.

To know how many people start a trial and subscribe, we report completed purchases to RevenueCat, a subscription-analytics provider. What RevenueCat receives:

  • the purchase record Apple issued: which plan, when, whether it is still active or in a free trial, and the country of your Apple Account;
  • a random identifier for your installation of the app; and
  • how far through the welcome screens you went, so we can see where they lose people.

RevenueCat is never given your name, your email address, your payment details, your answers on the welcome screens or anything from Apple Health.

Advertising for Stepnaut

Stepnaut shows no ads. We may advertise Stepnaut itself on the App Store, and an ad is only worth paying for if we can tell whether it brought anyone. So when the app is first opened it asks Apple whether the install followed a tap on one of our App Store ads. Apple's answer names the campaign and the search term, never the person, and it is kept by RevenueCat against the random identifier described under Subscriptions.

Stepnaut does not ask for permission to track you, does not use your device's advertising identifier, and includes no advertising company's software.

Notifications and Live Activities

Reminders, arrival alerts and your Lock Screen countdown are created on your iPhone and do not pass through our servers.

Children

Stepnaut does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.
